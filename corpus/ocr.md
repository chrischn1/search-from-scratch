# Working with scanned sources

A scanned book is an image until software reads it. Optical character recognition
turns the image into text, but it fails in predictable ways: broken letters, lost
diacritics, and columns read in the wrong order. Landscape scans that hold two
printed pages on one sheet are the worst case, because the software interleaves
the two columns and reports no error at all. Always check a page of prose by eye
before trusting the file.
