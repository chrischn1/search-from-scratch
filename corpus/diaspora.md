# Diaspora writing

Writers who live away from the place they write about carry two audiences at
once. The reader at home checks the work for accuracy. The reader abroad reads it
as a window. Migration, exile and return appear again and again in this fiction,
and so does the problem of language: the author often writes in the language of
the new country about the life of the old one. Hong Kong, Taiwan and the wider
Chinese speaking world produce a large body of such work.
